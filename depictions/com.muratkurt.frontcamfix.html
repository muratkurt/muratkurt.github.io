FrontCamFix

Front camera broken? FrontCamFix gives every app the rear camera instead: FaceTime, video calls, selfie and ID checks keep working. Also handy when an app only offers the front camera. The front camera is never started.

  • Every app: the switch happens in iOS's camera service, so Camera, FaceTime, video calls and Safari all get the rear camera. The app is not told.
  • Mirroring: stock (as the app sets it), off, or on.
  • Excluded apps keep the real front camera.

Settings › FrontCamFix. Changes apply the next time an app opens the camera. 12 languages.

Requirements

  • iOS 16 or 17 (iOS 18 and later not tested yet)
  • RootHide or a rootless jailbreak, PreferenceLoader, AltList
