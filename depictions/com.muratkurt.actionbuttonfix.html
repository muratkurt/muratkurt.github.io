ActionButtonFix

Separate actions for single and double click on the Action Button. Long press stays as Apple set it, and the Silent / hold pill no longer shows on a click.

Actions

  • Control: any Control Center control, picked in Apple's sheet (iOS 18+)
  • Accessibility: Zoom, Magnifier, VoiceOver and the rest of Apple's list
  • Shortcut: runs without opening Shortcuts, like Apple's button does
  • Siri
  • Voice Memo: start and stop a recording, also locked or with the screen off
  • Camera: front or back, any mode, optional auto record. Works from the Lock Screen
  • Screen Recording: same as Control Center's, countdown included
  • Control Center (iOS 17): Dark Mode, Low Power, Rotation Lock, Quick Note, Timer and more
  • Flashlight: four brightness levels. Volume buttons adjust it while it's on
  • Screenshot: stock flow, works with Prism and Snapper 3
  • Media: play/pause, next, previous
  • App: any app. From the Lock Screen it asks for Face ID first
  • Stock Action: the long press action, on a click
  • None: click off. With double click on None, single click fires instantly

When it runs

  • By Situation: other actions on the Lock Screen and in apps you choose
  • While Media Plays: media controls while something is playing
  • Condition (beta): another action while charging, on Wi-Fi, with headphones, while music plays or in a time range

Each click has its own haptic switch. A fresh install starts off, both clicks on None.

Requirements

  • iPhone with an Action Button, iOS 17 or later
  • RootHide or rootless
  • AltList (opa334's repo) and PreferenceLoader
