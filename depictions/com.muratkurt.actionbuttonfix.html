ActionButtonFix

Give the Action Button a single click and a double click of its own — without losing the long press.

What it does

On stock iOS the Action Button only responds to a long press. ActionButtonFix adds two more gestures on top of it:

  • Single click — runs an action you choose
  • Double click — runs a different action you choose
  • Long press — keeps whatever Apple already had assigned

The short press is never handed to Apple, so the usual Silent / hold feedback in the Dynamic Island doesn't start. Only your action runs.

Actions

  • Flashlight
  • Camera
  • Video Recording
  • Screenshot
  • Open an app — any system or App Store app
  • Stock Action — whatever you set for the long press in iOS Settings

Pick them separately for single and double click in Settings › ActionButtonFix.

Behaviour worth knowing

Video Recording

Opens the camera straight in video mode and starts recording on its own. It goes through Apple's own flow, so it is fast, and it works with the screen off and on the Lock Screen. The long-press action you chose in iOS Settings is untouched.

If you would rather press record yourself, turn off Start Recording Automatically.

Camera

Opens the native lock screen camera while the device is locked. The display wakes on its own, even from a black screen. On the Home Screen, Camera comes straight to the front instead.

Open an app

Picks from a searchable list of everything installed. It does not open apps from the Lock Screen — unlock first.

Screenshot

Uses the stock flow: flash, small preview in the bottom corner, tap it to edit. Left alone it disappears on its own. While the screen is off it does nothing, on purpose — waking the display only to capture the lock screen isn't worth it.

Flashlight

Goes through the same controller Control Center uses, so the two stay in sync. Turn it on with the button, turn it off from Control Center, or the other way round.

Haptic feedback

On by default, with separate switches for single and double click. Changing the action does not reset the switch.

Video Recording and Stock Action get their haptic from Apple, so the switch does not affect those two.

Requirements

  • iPhone 15 Pro or Pro Max — the Action Button only exists on these
  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • RootHide or a rootless jailbreak
  • AltList (opa334's repo) and PreferenceLoader

Tested on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.0.3, RootHide. The rootless package has not been tested on a device; other devices and iOS versions have not been verified on hardware.

Changelog

2.3.1

New

  • Video Recording action — a single or double press opens the camera directly in video mode and starts recording automatically; works with the screen off and on the Lock Screen
  • Start Recording Automatically switch — turn it off to start recording yourself
  • App action — assign any app to single and double press separately, from a searchable list
  • Stock Action — run the long-press action from iOS Settings with a single or double press
  • Haptic feedback — separate switches for single and double press, on by default
  • 12 languages — Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic
  • Rootless package — alongside RootHide

Improvements

  • Installs directly on RootHide without any extra tool
  • More stable Camera action
  • Cleaner Settings page; Video Recording and its switch have their own section
  • Smaller package

Known limitations

  • The App action does not open apps from the Lock Screen; unlock first
  • For Video Recording and Stock Action the haptic comes from Apple
  • The rootless package has not been tested on a device

2.2.0

First public release.

  • Screenshot now uses the stock flow — flash, preview in the corner, tap to edit. It used to save straight to Photos with no interface at all.
  • Camera opens properly from the Home Screen. It no longer leaves a hidden session running in the background.
  • Flashlight stays in sync with Control Center and can be switched off from there.
  • Screenshot does nothing while the screen is off, by design.
  • New icon and a tidied Settings page.
