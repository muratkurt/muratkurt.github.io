ActionButtonFix

Single click and double click for the Action Button. The long press stays Apple's.

Gestures

  • Single click — an action you pick
  • Double click — a different action you pick
  • Long press — unchanged, whatever iOS Settings has

The short press is never handed to Apple, so the Silent / hold feedback in the Dynamic Island never appears. Only your action runs.

Actions

Camera

Side (Back / Front) and mode (Video, Slo-Mo, Time-Lapse, Cinematic, Photo, Portrait), set per click. Optionally starts recording as it opens; the same click stops it. Opens through Apple's own flow, so it is fast and works with the screen off and on the Lock Screen.

Screen Recording

Starts and stops the recording. Same as Control Center's: 3-2-1 countdown, Dynamic Island view, saved to Photos. A recording started in Control Center stops with the button, and the other way round. The microphone follows the Control Center setting.

Control Center

Runs one module: Dark Mode, Rotation Lock, Silent Mode, Low Power Mode, QR Code Reader, Magnifier, Voice Memos, Quick Note, Stopwatch, Timer, Alarm, Calculator, Wallet.

Flashlight

The same controller Control Center uses, so the two stay in sync.

Screenshot

The stock flow: flash, corner preview, tap to edit. Does nothing while the screen is off.

App

Any installed app, from a searchable list. Does not open apps from the Lock Screen.

Stock Action

The long-press action from iOS Settings, on a click.

None

Turns a click off. With double click set to None, the single click fires instantly, with no wait.

Settings

Each click is configured on its own in Settings › ActionButtonFix, action and options both.

Haptic feedback has separate switches for single and double click, on by default, and survives an action change.

Camera, Screen Recording and Stock Action get their haptic from Apple, so the switch does not reach those three.

On a fresh install the tweak is off and both clicks are None. Camera starts at the back camera, photo mode.

Settings follow your device language.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with an Action Button — 15 Pro / Pro Max, and the 16 line
  • iOS 17 or later
  • RootHide or a rootless jailbreak
  • AltList (opa334's repo) and PreferenceLoader

Tested on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.0.3, RootHide. The rootless package is built and shipped the same way, not verified on the developer's device. iOS 18 and the iPhone 16 line are untested.
