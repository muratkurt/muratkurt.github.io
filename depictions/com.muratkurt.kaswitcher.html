KASwitcher

Close all apps in the app switcher at once.

Features

  • Close all — hold the X button, tap Close All
  • Undo — tap the X during the countdown
  • Pin — hold a card; pinned apps are not closed
  • Protect — music and maps, plus any apps you choose
  • Keep the most recent app open
  • Cascade animation — style and speed
  • Move and resize the X button, portrait and landscape

Languages

Türkçe · English · Deutsch · Français · Español · Italiano · Português · Русский · 简体中文 · 日本語 · 한국어 · العربية

Requirements

  • iOS 16 or later
  • RootHide or rootless
  • PreferenceLoader
