AppTrackerBlock

Blocks app trackers system-wide, Safari included. Nothing is injected into apps.

See what was blocked in the app, the quick panel and the widgets; a daily report comes at 21:00. The Control Center button is built in on iOS 18+; on iOS 15–17 it needs CCSupport.
