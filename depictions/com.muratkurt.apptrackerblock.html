AppTrackerBlock

Blocks app trackers system-wide, Safari included. Nothing is injected into apps.

Features

  • Blocking — tracker domains are dropped in the network layer, for every app
  • App — Overview, Apps, Trackers, Live and Settings: today, the last 7 and 30 days, every app, company, category and server country, a real-time feed, CSV export
  • Your rules — allow a domain everywhere or in one app, block your own domains, pause one app for 10 minutes; blocking and Deep mode per app
  • Daily report — at 21:00: what was blocked, and which apps reached trackers for the first time
  • Quick panel — from Control Center; pause for 15 minutes or an hour
  • Widgets — Home Screen (9 themes) and Lock Screen; Shortcuts
  • Deep mode — optional, for apps with their own network code
  • 12 languages

How it works

  • A small background service places drop rules in iOS's own network policy system (NECP) — the layer iOS uses for its own network rules.
  • Each rule is a tracker domain: about 2,600, where HaGeZi Multi PRO meets the DuckDuckGo and Disconnect company lists. The list updates itself once a week.
  • A connection to one of them is dropped before it leaves the phone, in every app and in Safari; system lookups are cut too.
  • Nothing is injected into apps, so they keep running untouched. Apps you exclude and domains you allow are skipped.
  • Each blocked attempt is counted on the device with the app, the company and the server's country.
  • Pause or turn it off and the rules are gone within milliseconds; nothing is left behind.
  • Apps that bring their own DNS or a fixed IP get past the system layer; Deep mode covers those inside the apps you pick.

Requirements

  • iOS 15 or later, RootHide or rootless
  • PreferenceLoader
  • CCSupport, only for the Control Center button on iOS 15–17 (built in on iOS 18+)
